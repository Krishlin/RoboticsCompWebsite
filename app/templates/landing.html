Summit

Push your opponent off the hill to win. Otherwise, the robot higher up when time runs out wins. Play too aggressively and you might fall off. Play too safely and you might lose on height.

Constraints
Start within 10 × 10 cm and weigh no more than 500 g. No height limit, and expansion is allowed after the start.
Controller
One micro:bit runs your code. The rest of the design is yours, within the rules.
Match
Up to three minutes. Both robots start together.
Tournament
At least six best-of-three qualification series per team. Top-ranked teams advance to eliminations.

The Hill

Five colored zones help your robot detect where it is. Use a color sensor to navigate toward the summit or avoid driving off the edge.

The kit is a Wukong expansion board with a built-in battery, two continuous-rotation motors, an ultrasonic sensor for finding the opponent, a colour sensor for reading the zone, LEGO Technic chassis parts, and the starter code. It is programmed in MakeCode. The micro:bit is required and sold separately.

The optional kit gets you to a working robot quickly. The interesting part begins when you change it and see whether your idea works against another team.
